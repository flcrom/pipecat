- Added `ZoomSTTService` and `ZoomHttpSTTService`, speech-to-text with Zoom Scribe. Install them with `pipecat-ai[zoom-ai]`. `pipecat init` offers `ZoomSTTService` as "Zoom".
  - `ZoomSTTService` streams audio to Scribe Live. Scribe's voice activity detection ends each turn, so the service recommends `ExternalUserTurnStrategies`. `Settings.silence_duration_ms` (default 350, tuned for voice agents) sets how much silence ends a turn.
  - `ZoomHttpSTTService` sends each utterance cut by the pipeline's VAD to Scribe Fast. `Settings.word_time_offsets` adds word timings.
  - On both, `Settings.diarization` (off by default) labels speakers; the labels are in `TranscriptionFrame.result`.
